## 3. What you get · who it's for

- **Eyebrow:** What you get · who it's for
- **H2:** A system, *not just a logo.*
- **Lead:** Plenty of businesses are better than they look. A brand closes that gap. We build a complete identity system (logo, colour, type, voice) so every touchpoint, from your van to your website, says the same thing in the same way.
- **"In every brand"** (list, one line each):
  - **Logo design and all its variations.** The main mark plus the versions you need for small spaces, dark backgrounds and social profiles.
  - **Typography system and font licensing guidance.** Which fonts, where, and what you're allowed to use them for.
  - **Colour palette and usage rules.** The colours, and when each one is used.
  - **Brand guidelines document.** The rules written down, so the next person who makes something gets it right.
  - **Social media templates.** Posts your team fills in without breaking the look.
  - **Print and packaging design.** Signage, stationery and packaging in the same system.
- **"Good fit if"**:
  - You've moved up-market and your brand hasn't
  - Your materials look different every time someone new makes them
  - You're launching something new and want it right the first time

## 7. Process and price

- **Eyebrow:** Process and timeline
- **H2:** Same five steps. *The length changes.*
- **Steps:**
  1. **Short call.** What you sell, who buys it and what's not working today.
  2. **Positioning.** Who you're for and what they should feel, agreed before any design.
  3. **The system.** Logo, colour and type, designed together.
  4. **The rules.** Guidelines and templates, written down so anyone can use them.
  5. **Hand over.** Final files your team can use for years. The IP is yours on full payment.

## 9. FAQ (5)

- **Eyebrow:** FAQ · 05 · **H2:** Questions about brand identity

1. **How long does a brand identity project take?** Brand identity projects typically take 4–6 weeks, from the positioning brief to the final guidelines handover. A full brand and website together take longer; Cleen & Green took 8 weeks.
2. **How much does a brand identity cost?** We quote brand work per project, once we know the scope: a logo refresh is a different job from a full system with guidelines, templates and packaging. A brand plus website, like Cleen & Green, is custom work, typically between $5,000 and $20,000.
3. **What does a brand identity include?** Logo design and all its variations, a typography system with font licensing guidance, a colour palette with usage rules, a brand guidelines document, social media templates, and print and packaging design.
4. **Do you start with the logo?** No. We start with positioning: who you are for and what they should feel. Then we design the system, write the rules down and hand you files your team can use for years.
5. **Who owns the brand files?** You do. On full payment, all intellectual property in the final deliverables is assigned to you.
