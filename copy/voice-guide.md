# Fazed Digital voice guide (service pages)

The Wordsmith · 10 Nov 2026 · copyStatus: draft

Drawn from the live copy in `src/data/services.ts` (chapters), `src/data/lp.ts` (7-day FAQ), `src/pages/contact.astro`, `src/pages/audit.astro` and `src/data/case-seo.ts`.

1. **Short declaratives, then the reason.** "No lock-in." "We'll recommend the right fit, not the one we like best." One idea per sentence. Average 12–16 words.
2. **The reader's problem first, in their words.** "Your site looks older than your business." "A feature nobody can find is a feature nobody uses." Fit lines start with "You" or "Your".
3. **Concrete nouns over adjectives.** Name the thing: "a quote request flow", "chauffeur scheduling", "a four-step enquiry form". Never "solutions", "elevate", "unlock", "seamless", "cutting-edge".
4. **Numbers are real or absent.** Weeks from `cases.ts`, prices from `proof.ts` (or the owner's 10 Nov decisions, once added there). No percentages, no "x times", no results we cannot show.
5. **Honest limits, said plainly.** "No one can promise rankings." "It is a private, staff-only system, so there is no public link." When we have no client proof, we say what we run on our own business instead.
6. **Ownership and next steps are always explicit.** "The domain, hosting, logins and files are yours." "A real person replies within one business day."
7. **House style.** UK spelling (optimise, colour). En dash for ranges (4–12 weeks). No em dashes. Colons introduce lists. Buttons say what happens next ("Scope a system", "Book a free 15-minute automation check"), never "Submit" or "Learn more".
8. **Two-line H1 rhythm.** Line 1 names the service with a full stop. Line 2 is the outcome, under ~40 characters so it stays on one line at desktop.
