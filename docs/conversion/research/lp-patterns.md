## 4. Ten changes, ranked by expected impact

Each item names the pattern it comes from. None needs testimonials, logos, timers or invented numbers.

**1. Make Dotted Studios the lead proof, directly under the hero.** *(Patterns 10 + 11; Flowout and Goodspeed hero case chips)*
Layout: a full-width "Latest launch" case band as screen 2, before the process. Show the screenshot (`work-dotted.jpg`) in a browser frame at about 60% width. Beside it, on a mono data rail: client · Iligan City · Web design + CMS · "Live one week after the build started" · Oct 2026. Add an honest caveat in the body ("built from the studio's own Figma design"). In the hero, add a small proof chip under the trust strip ("Latest launch: live in 1 week ↓") that scrolls to the band. The chip stays on-page, so the funnel rule holds. Drop the "not 7-day sites" disclaimer from the intro, because one real case now backs the promise.

**2. Turn the client results into image-led case cards, ordered by relevance to a 7-day buyer.** *(Patterns 10 + 11; theCSS Problem/Outcome cards, Penji paired quote+metric cards)*
Replace the 3 text cards with a 2×2 grid. Each card gets a screenshot, a country/sector tag, the build duration from cases.ts and the existing outcome headline. Lead with the closest matches: A Framing Company (US small-business marketing site), Cleen & Green (PH), The Telecom Shop (AU), then CrateOnScene. Print the live domain as plain text (verifiable, not a link, so it stays inside the funnel rule). Put a "Get my start date" CTA directly under this block. Shrink the Lab section to a secondary "craft" strip lower down: it is fictional, and it currently outranks real clients.

**3. Attach the guarantee to the price.** *(Pattern 9; Designjoy and Penji put risk reversal beside the plans)*
Inside the pricing section, add a single guarantee rail under the three cards, with one row of four marks: 7 business days or 20% off · No payment until you approve the quote · Deposit refundable before work starts · You own everything. Then move the lime promise band so it sits directly **before** pricing. It then works as the bridge from proof to price, not as an afterthought after it. On mobile, the rail stacks as a 2×2 grid inside the same section.

**4. Fix the PH price in the docked capsule and unify the CTA label.** *(Patterns 5 + 1)*
Header.astro line 35 hardcodes `₱19,999`. Read it from `offerPrice('php','starter')` so the sticky capsule matches the founding ₱14,999 shown everywhere else. This bar stays on screen for the whole scroll, so the mismatch is the most visible inconsistency on /ph. Rename the capsule button "Get started" to "Get my start date" so every CTA says the same thing.

**5. Add a typographic client-name row under the hero trust strip.** *(Pattern 8, adapted to "no logo permission")*
One hairline-separated mono line: "Built for" + client names with country flags or codes (The Telecom Shop AU · CrateOnScene US · Cleen & Green PH · A Framing Company US · Dotted Studios PH …). Use names only, no logos. These names are already public on /work. Have the owner confirm that no client has asked not to be named.

**6. Add a founder "who you'll work with" block beside the form.** *(Minority pattern: founder face; Designjoy and Goodspeed)*
In the `.lp-form-side` column, above the pills, add a portrait (`/images/team/lord.jpeg`), name, role and one line ("You talk to the people who design and build your site"), plus the studio clock already in the header. A real face is the most honest substitute for testimonials we have. Repeat a small version of the avatar on the form's final "Where do we send your start date?" step, next to the reply promise.

**7. Offer a second, human path next to the brief.** *(Pattern 4)*
Keep the brief as the primary action. Under the form card and at the end of the FAQ, add a ghost button: PH "Prefer Messenger or Viber? Message us", USD "Prefer to talk first? Book a 15-minute call". Add it only for channels the owner actually staffs within the stated reply time, and tag it so the CRM tracks it separately. Without this, visitors who don't want to fill in a form have no way in except a small mailto link.

**8. Turn the Day-1 condition into a visible checklist and add fit / not-a-fit.** *(Pattern 6 + minority fit pattern; Diamond Heart, BuildSprig)*
Under the 5-step process, add a two-column panel. Left: "What you send on Day 1", with 4 tick rows (logo, photos, page text, 2–3 sites you like) plus the PH "or just your Facebook page" shortcut. Right: "A great fit / Scoped separately" (fit: 5–10 page business sites, service businesses, shops with up to X products if the owner confirms; separate: booking systems, portals, custom e-commerce). This turns the guarantee's conditions from fine print into a confidence builder, and it filters out bad-fit briefs before they reach the inbox.

**9. Widen the comparison to the buyer's real alternatives.** *(Minority comparison pattern; Goodspeed and theCSS compare 3–4 options)*
Change the 2-column table to 4 columns: DIY builder · Freelancer marketplace · Typical agency · Fazed (PH: swap DIY for "Facebook page only"). Use qualitative rows only (who designs it, time to live, price certainty, ownership, who answers when it breaks), with no competitor prices. On mobile (375px), render it as a segmented switcher: one alternative vs Fazed at a time, with the Fazed column pinned. Don't make it a sideways-scrolling table.

**10. Add a 60–90 second founder walkthrough of a real build.** *(Minority video pattern; ManyPixels, Kimp, Flowout, Penji demo)*
A captioned, click-to-play screen recording: the brief, a Day 2–3 mockup, the staging link, the live site (Dotted Studios). Place it in the process section, with a poster frame and lazy loading so it doesn't affect LCP. It is real footage, so it proves the process without needing a review. Then wire a review slot to the founding-client deal: when the first approved quotes and a Google Business Profile rating exist, flip `LP_REVIEWS_REAL` and show the rating next to the CTA under the hero (patterns 3 + 7). Until then, show nothing in that slot.

### Suggested new scroll story
Hero (price, CTA, risk line, proof chip) → Latest launch: Dotted Studios → Client work grid + CTA → Process + Day-1 checklist / fit → 7-day promise → Pricing + guarantee rail → Comparison → Lab (craft strip) → FAQ → Founder + form (+ human path). PH keeps "Facebook vs website" after the latest-launch band.

### Explicitly not recommended
Countdown timers, "only N spots left" counters (proof.ts: no live counter), star ratings, review widgets, logo walls or "as featured in" strips. None of these is backed by real data yet. A scoped capacity line already exists (15 builds a month, 5 founding spots). Show remaining founding spots only if the owner updates the number by hand when a spot is taken (Goodspeed's month-scoped line is the honest version).
