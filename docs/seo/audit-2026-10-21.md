### Critical

**C1 · Invented client story in a live post**
- **Evidence:** `/blog/outsource-web-development-philippines` says: "a Utah framing company came to us after a local agency quoted $8,000 for a six-page site. They chose our 7-day website package, and the site launched in under two weeks." `src/data/cases.ts:18` says A Framing Company was a **6-week** WordPress project (2024). There is no $8,000 quote and no 7-day package in the case data. This breaks the routine's own rule (`routine-prompt.md:67`: "never invent Fazed client results").
- **Impact:** A false, quotable claim on a page built to be cited by AI engines. It is a trust and legal risk if the client sees it, and it contradicts `/work/01`, so entity facts no longer agree.
- **Fix:** Replace the paragraph with the facts from `cases.ts`: "A Framing Company, a Utah framing contractor, needed a site that showed their finished builds. We designed and built a portfolio-led WordPress site in 6 weeks, and it is now a core part of how they pitch new work." Keep the `/work/01` link. Editing this entry touches the `posts` arrays, which the CLAUDE.md contract forbids, so **Lord must approve a one-off edit** (keep both arrays byte-identical), or ask the routine to fix its own entry.
- **Owner / effort:** Lord approves, us edits · S

**C2 · The same post presents Fazed's own prices as market data**
- **Evidence:** "Studios there typically charge $499–$1,499… These are general ranges from published agency price guides." $499/$899/$1,499 are exactly our packages (`proof.ts` `prices.usd`). No guide is cited, and the directory data found today says otherwise (DesignRush lists PH studios at $9–$15/h with $1,000+ minimums; GoodFirms reports a $25/h median).
- **Impact:** A misattributed number that AI answers may repeat as an industry fact.
- **Fix:** "Our own fixed-price packages are $499, $899 and $1,499. Philippine studios that list rates on directories such as GoodFirms and DesignRush mostly quote $10–$25 an hour, so always ask for a fixed quote." Fix this in the same edit as C1.
- **Owner / effort:** us (with C1) · S
