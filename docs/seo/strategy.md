# Fazed Digital: SEO strategy (Oct 2026 – Apr 2027)

Prepared 19 Oct 2026. Scope: organic search for www.fazeddigital.com in four markets (Philippines, USA, UK, Australia), aimed at **enquiries and booked calls**, not traffic for its own sake.
Facts used about Fazed come only from `src/data/` (site.ts, proof.ts, cases.ts, services.ts, lp.ts), the live page files and `/terms`. Unverified proof stats (120+ projects, 98%, 4x ROI, 12+ sites, 100% on-time, 7x faster) are **not** used anywhere in this plan.

---

## 1. Where we start (honest baseline)

- **New domain in Google's eyes.** Site rebuilt Oct 2026, DNS moved mid-October, few backlinks. Expect 2–8 weeks before Google settles on the new URLs. A web search for `fazeddigital.com` on 19 Oct 2026 returned only look-alike brands (Faze Digital London, FZP Digital, Fuze Digital), not our site, so **brand searches are not safe yet** either.
- **What we have that most small studios don't:** published fixed prices in two currencies, a dated 7-day guarantee, 8 real case studies (USA ×2, Australia ×4, Philippines ×2), a strong technical base (fast static site, schema on every page, llms.txt, sitemaps) and 40 blog posts.
- **What we don't have:** reviews (LP reviews are placeholders), verified result metrics, any UK client, any named Iligan or Cagayan de Oro client, links from other sites.
- **Implication:** in the first 3–6 months the wins come from (1) local Mindanao searches where the page-one results are weak directory listings, (2) long-tail buyer questions where our own prices and cases make us the most specific answer, and (3) low-competition niches where we have a real case (Magento → PrestaShop, membership portals, law-firm client portals). National head terms ("web design philippines", "small business website cost") are 6–12+ month goals.

## 2. What the search results show (observed 19 Oct 2026)

Method: live web searches (US-based engine) plus Google autocomplete pulled per country (`suggestqueries.google.com`, `gl=ph/us/gb/au`). Autocomplete shows that people search a phrase; it does not give volumes. Get volumes from Search Console once impressions arrive, or Google Keyword Planner. Sources are listed in section 11.
